# The gate — every video, before upload

**must** = the video does not upload until it passes. **should** = fix unless there is a reason, and
write the reason down.

## Content

| # | level | item |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | must | Every claim traces to a sentence in the approved article. No outside health facts. |
| 2 | must | Hook within 1.5 s, spoken and on screen, with different words in each. |
| 3 | must | The promise says what is coming and how many. |
| 4 | must | One call to action only, in the last 6 s. |
| 5 | should | The last line leads back into the first (loop). |
| 6 | should | Length 25–40 s. Longer needs a reason. |

## Brand frame

| # | level | item |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | must | Navy dominant, blue emphasis, teal one touch. No fourth colour. |
| 8 | must | Tajawal everywhere; captions 2–3 words, 800 weight, navy stroke. |
| 9 | must | Caption word order correct — check a frame (no colour override mid-line). |
| 10 | must | Modonty icon top-right; series tag on body frames. |
| 11 | must | The article's own images; no generic AI imagery. |
| 12 | must | Client or doctor credit with the trust shield. |
| 13 | must | Standard end card + sonic logo. |
| 14 | should | Picture changes at least every 3 s. |
| 15 | should | All text inside the safe area (y 180–1480, side margins 140). |

## Upload

| # | level | item |
|---|---|---|
| 16 | must | Uploaded **private**; a person publishes. |
| 17 | must | "Altered or synthetic content" ticked when the voice is synthetic. |
| 18 | must | Title has the series name; description's first two lines answer the question; article link has UTM. |

## Regular video only

| # | level | item |
|---|---|---|
| 19 | must | No intro: the first 10 s deliver the title's promise. |
| 20 | must | Chapter cards between chapters; chapters in the description with 00:00 first. |
| 21 | must | End card beat at least 8 s, left half clear for end-screen elements. |
| 22 | must | Thumbnail on the fixed template. |
| 23 | must | Each cut Short: new hook line, «الفيديو الكامل» call, Related Video → the anchor. |

After publishing, the growth actions in `growth.md` — they are not optional, they are where the
subscribers come from.
