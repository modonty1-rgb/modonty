# The five series

Five shows, one brand frame. Each has its own beat sheet and a recognisable opening, so a returning
viewer knows which show it is in the first second. Repeat what works: a series whose Shorts run at
2x the channel's average views gets more slots the next week (see `growth.md`).

**Rotation rule:** never the same series two days running. One article can feed several series
over the weeks — never two Shorts from the same article in the same week.

---

## 1 · «٣ علامات» — the list

**Use when** the article lists signs, symptoms, reasons or types.
**Signature open:** a large «٣» fills the screen, then shrinks into the corner as a counter.

| beat | content |
|---|---|
| Hook | the situation the viewer is in, not the topic: «تفتح الجوال تشوف مقطع… وتطلع بعد ساعة؟» |
| Promise | «ثلاث علامات، الثالثة أغلب الناس ما ينتبه لها» |
| 1, 2, 3 | one sign each, counter ticks ٣ → ٢ → ١, the last is the least obvious |
| Call | «لو عندك ثنتين منها، شوف الفيديو الكامل» → related long video |

## 2 · «صح ولا خطأ؟» — the myth

**Use when** the article corrects a common belief.
**Signature open:** the claim on screen with two buttons «صح» «خطأ», a 1-second pause, viewer guesses.

| beat | content |
|---|---|
| Hook | the belief stated as fact, as people say it |
| Promise | «أغلب الناس يجاوبون غلط» |
| Reveal | the answer with a blue box, then the one-line reason from the article |
| Why it matters | what happens if you act on the wrong belief |
| Call | «اكتب في التعليقات: كنت تعرفها؟» → comment, and add a Quiz sticker in Studio |

## 3 · «رقم يصدمك» — the number

**Use when** the article has one striking, sourced figure.
**Signature open:** the number counts up full-screen (Tajawal 900, 230 px).

| beat | content |
|---|---|
| Hook | the number with no context: «١٠٠ ألف شخص» |
| Promise | «وش طلع معهم يخصك أنت» |
| Context | what the number measures, and the source named out loud |
| Meaning | what it means for the viewer in one sentence |
| Call | article link: «المصدر والتفاصيل في المقالة» |

## 4 · «سوّها كذا» — the steps

**Use when** the article gives practical steps or a routine.
**Signature open:** a short "before" frame (the problem) then a hard cut to step one.

| beat | content |
|---|---|
| Hook | the result first: «من الليلة تنام أبكر بخطوة وحدة» |
| Steps | 2–3 steps, each a verb first: «وقّف… حدّد… أبعد…» |
| Proof | why it works, one line from the article |
| Call | «احفظ المقطع وجرّبها الليلة» → save, and the related long video |

## 5 · «اسأل الدكتور» — the doctor

**Use when** the client sent a phone clip of the doctor answering the article's question. The most
authentic format and the strongest for the client relationship; give it a slot every week a clip exists.
**Signature open:** the question as a chat bubble, then the doctor on screen.

| beat | content |
|---|---|
| Hook | the patient's question, word for word as people type it |
| Answer | the doctor, trimmed with `yt-edit` (dead air out), captions burned in |
| Credit | doctor's name, title and clinic with the trust shield, held 3 s |
| Call | «احجز من رابط المقالة» → article |

## Where the series live in the regular video

Each series is also a chapter shape inside the regular video (see `long.md`): the anchor is built out
of these chapters, and its Shorts are those chapters cut out, re-hooked and re-laid vertically. A
Short written from scratch, without an anchor, is the exception — only for timely topics that cannot
wait for an anchor.
